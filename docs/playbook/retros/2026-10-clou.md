# Retro: Clou, 2026-10

Clou (sports video app), 22 to 28 Oct 2026: concept, plan, about 350 designed screens in 13 rounds, an Expo SDK 58 skeleton with CI, no coded screen. Updated by the v2, v3 and v3.1 reviews; fixes are marked **(corrected)**.

## Why it stopped (corrected 28 Oct 2026, Sebastian)
In Sebastian's words, Clou did not stop because the scope kept growing. It stopped because the design could not be turned into a working app: how to program it, how the repo works and how to get from design to code were unclear and much harder than he expected. The scope and gate rows below are true as facts, but they were not why it stopped. He wants to rethink the Clou idea later.

## Goal and mode at the start
Mode: never chosen (meant as a startup, run like a learning project). Appetite: none. Kill/expand criteria: none; Gate 1 (demand check) was dropped.

## Was it a good idea?
We don't know: **no market signal either way**. No talk with a stranger, no signup page, no reach channel. Untested: a feed needs creators and viewers at once; a solo founder can't win on feature count against CapCut (E). The planned TikTok channel was the cheapest demand test and never started.
**Fix:** decisions 3 and 34: pick a mode on day 0; in Business mode, Reach comes first and evidence blocks scope growth, not coding.

## Two kinds of lessons
- **Tool lessons** (monorepo, Figma quota, Dependabot, cloud branches, Expo Go SDK, Windows safety) were learned well: each now has a setting, file or troubleshooting row.
- **Behaviour lessons** were learned only on paper. Clou's decision log was excellent: "Gate 1 dropped" and "no feature timeline" are in it, with reasons. Platform choices were reversed within hours.
**Fix:** behaviour gets mechanics, not text: tools fixed for the slice (7); teach-back in every wrap-up (28). **(28 Oct 2026)** Waivers and the hour cap were removed again (49).

## Surprises (facts that were wrong or changed)
- **Expo Go store lag.** The App Store Expo Go was on 57 while Clou used 58; it moved to 58.0.5 about 23 Oct (E). In 2026 SDK 56 apparently never reached the App Store (E). **(corrected)** v2 made "pin to the store SDK" the default; v3 keeps SDK 58 and checks the App Store listing on day 0 (9).
- **The safety layer did less than it said.** v1 missed PowerShell and nested `.env`. v2 guard: 70 of 74 bypasses passed, and `git push -fu` force-pushed (tested during v3). **(corrected)** The guard is a seatbelt against accidents, not a wall; safety comes from exact allow rules, no production secrets on the PC, and the cloud for unattended work (24).
- **Manual mode is not safety.** People approve about 93% of prompts. It is a learning tool for week 1 (25).
- **Overnight runs (corrected).** Now cloud only, with a machine check, a turn cap and a diff read in the morning (11).
