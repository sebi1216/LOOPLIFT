Created by /kickoff when Business mode was chosen. Newest first, 3 lines per entry.
Evidence never blocks coding. It decides growing scope (slice 2, a backend, a new platform).
Targets: one named channel with a number; 5 Mom Test conversations (not friends or family); 1 smoke test page with a success number set before looking. No dates: Sebastian sets the pace.

## Mom Test script (15 min, do not pitch until the end)
1. Tell me about the last time you <did the job>. What happened?
2. What was the hardest part? What did you do about it?
3. What have you tried or paid for? Why did you stop?
4. How much time or money does this cost you now?
5. (End) I am building something for this. Can I show you when it works?
